Executive Overview

Short-form video platforms—YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok—have fundamentally changed content discovery. However, repurposing long-form widescreen videos (podcasts, tutorials, conference keynotes) into high-retention 9:16 vertical shorts manually requires hours of timestamp hunting, manual cropping, and subtitle alignment.

Existing cloud services charge steep monthly subscriptions and enforce cloud watermarks. clipYT is a standalone desktop web application featuring a high-performance FastAPI backend and modern dark glassmorphic web UI. It operates with zero external workflow orchestration (zero n8n required) and runs directly on local hardware or private containers.

Core Innovation: An integrated end-to-end media DSP pipeline combining Gemini LLM semantic virality scoring, NVIDIA Canary-1B high-speed speech-to-text, complex FFmpeg blurred backdrop canvas filters, and safe-zone subtitle rendering.

Solving the Hard Video Engineering Challenges

Automating video reframing involves subtle visual and UX hurdles that simple scripts fail to handle:

The "Peephole" Problem

Blind center crops chop off presentation slides, co-hosts, and wide podcast tables, leaving viewers disoriented.

Subtitle Overflow & Blocking

Standard subtitles render across the entire screen width, getting covered by TikTok/Reels UI buttons and avatar sidebars.

Smart Aspect Ratio & Blurred Backdrop Engine

clipYT implements an advanced multi-pass FFmpeg filter_complex pipeline that resolves the framing dilemma:

1. Blurred Backdrop (Recommended)

Preserves 100% of the widescreen video in the vertical center while dynamically synthesizing a heavily blurred, scaled background layer behind it. Perfect for coding tutorials, podcasts, and gaming.

2. Center Fill (9:16)

Zooms and crops tightly to center screen. Ideal for single talking-head interviews and solo vloggers.

3. Native Landscape (16:9)

Preserves native widescreen proportions for Twitter/X video dispatches and desktop embeds.

FFmpeg Dual-Layer Blurred Backdrop Filter 
# FFmpeg Dual-Layer Blurred Backdrop Filter:
ffmpeg -i input.mp4 -filter_complex \
"[0:v]scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase,crop=1080:1920,boxblur=20:5[bg]; \
 [0:v]scale=1080:608:force_original_aspect_ratio=decrease[fg]; \
 [bg][fg]overlay=(W-w)/2:(H-h)/2" \
-c:a copy output_vertical.mp4

Word Burst Chunker & Safe Zone Captions

Captions are vital: over 75% of mobile users browse short-form videos with audio muted. clipYT introduces a Word Burst Chunker:

  • Pacing: Rather than dumping whole sentences onto the canvas, it segments cues into 3–5 word bursts (1–2 seconds each) mimicking TikTok creator pacing.
  • Safe Zone Enforcing: Positioned in the lower-third with strict margins:
    MarginL=80, MarginR=80, MarginV=260
    Words never clip beyond screen boundaries or collide with platform like/comment icons.
  • High-Contrast Typography: Vibrant yellow or crisp white font paired with thick black outlines and drop shadows, ensuring 100% legibility on bright or dark footage.
  • Top Hook Banner: Renders an attention-grabbing title banner at the top of the video during the first 5 seconds to maximize viewer retention.

Gemini AI Virality Engine

clipYT employs Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash (with automatic fallback to gemini-3.5-flash and gemini-3.1-flash-lite) to analyze transcript semantics:

Retention Scoring

Calculates virality scores (e.g. 96/100) based on emotional peaks, sudden reveals, and suspenseful hooks.

Hook Titles

Generates high-CTR video header copy crafted specifically for instant viewer intrigue.

Social Kit

Outputs ready-to-post descriptions, hashtags, and engagement prompts for YouTube Shorts, Reels, and TikTok.

Automatic Speech-to-Text via NVIDIA Canary-1B NIM

Many valuable YouTube videos lack native subtitles or have auto-captions disabled by creators. clipYT solves this seamlessly:

  1. Extracts the high-fidelity audio stream from YouTube via yt-dlp.
  2. Transmits the compressed audio chunks to NVIDIA Canary-1B-ASR NIM microservices.
  3. Executes rapid parallel speech-to-text inference with automatic punctuation and sentence segmentation.
  4. Generates word-aligned WebVTT caption tracks in seconds without human transcription.

Bypassing YouTube Bot Restrictions (cookies.txt)

YouTube employs aggressive anti-scraping and rate-limiting measures, frequently throwing "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" errors when downloading high-resolution streams.

clipYT integrates native Netscape-format cookie parsing:

Architecture & Real-Time Dashboard

The application is built on modern asynchronous primitives:

  • FastAPI Async Core: Non-blocking media processing and background subprocess management.
  • Server-Sent Events (SSE): 4-stage live progress bar communicating audio extraction, transcription, AI hook scoring, and FFmpeg video rendering.
  • In-Browser Video Player: Custom HTML5 video player with real-time aspect toggles.
  • One-Click Asset Copy: Copy buttons for viral hooks, social captions, and direct MP4 downloads.

Deployment & Quick Start

clipYT supports native execution and Docker containerization:

Execution & Deployment 
# Method 1: Native Python (Fastest & Recommended)
python run.py
# Server automatically opens http://127.0.0.1:8787 in your browser!

# Method 2: Docker Compose
docker compose up --build -d
